**The honest limit, stated (plan trap 14):** no grammar recovers an
already-merged prose-titled rescue whose commit predates this reader. The
trailer is written into the PR body at rescue time and is immutable afterwards,
exactly like the subject — so a rescue merged before this story (PR #297 is the
standing case) is not recoverable by any grammar, and one story of spec 088
stays permanently invisible to `spec landed` and to `--delta`. This story makes
future rescues survivable; it does not rewrite history.
